WHERE TO CHECK BEFORE YOU SHARE

THE VERIFY KIT

Fact-checkers who publish their methods, news wires that file the raw reports most outlets rewrite, and official data you can read for yourself. Bookmark it, print it, send it to the family group.

1. TWO-SOURCE RULENo single outlet is perfectly neutral. Confirm a claim with at least two independent sources before you share it.
2. GO TO THE ORIGINALRead the wire report, the official release or the actual dataset, then compare how different outlets framed it.
3. CHECK WHO CHECKEDGood fact-checkers show sources and corrections. Many sign the IFCN code; the current signatory list is here ↗.
